## Part II: Evidence of Tartary's Economic Independence

### 2.1 The Fur Road vs. Debt-Based Currency

The Tartary research documents an extensive **"northern fur road"** running through Siberian territories:

| Economic Feature | Tartary System | Babylonian/Roman System |
|------------------|----------------|-------------------------|
| Currency Basis | Commodity (furs, goods) | Debt instruments |
| Trade Networks | Silk Road, Kyakhta | Bond markets, tribute |
| Wealth Accumulation | Tangible assets | Abstract credit |
| Tax System | *Yasak* (fur tribute) | Monetary taxation |

**"Soft Gold" Independence:**
- Sable pelts functioned as de facto currency
- A single prime sable pelt exceeded a peasant family's annual income
- At its peak, fur trade accounted for 10%+ of Russian state revenue

**Implication:** Tartary operated a **resource-based economy** that did not require participation in the debt-based currency systems emanating from Babylon through Rome to modern banking networks.

### 2.2 Mineral and Energy Resources

From the Tartary cartographic research:
> "Pre-1750 maps show thousands of towns with permanent infrastructure, vast trading networks, and organized khanates with advanced governance."

The region possessed:
- **Extensive mineral wealth** (documented mining operations)
- **Energy resources** (later discoveries confirm massive oil/gas reserves in former "Tartary" regions)
- **Strategic trade routes** controlling East-West commerce

**Connection to Book of the Unrendered:**
The petrodollar system (1973-1974) was designed to create:
> "Inexhaustible demand for dollars—nations needed them to buy energy."

A region with **independent energy and mineral resources** represented an existential threat to this model. The elimination of "Independent Tartary" from maps correlates with the expansion of resource-control empires.
